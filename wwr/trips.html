Featured River Adventures

Main Salmon River Expedition

The legendary "River of No Return" cuts through the second-deepest canyon in North America. Enjoy rollicking Class III-IV rapids, wide sandy campsites, relaxing hot springs, and gourmet campfire dining under starry skies.

  • Duration: 4 Days / 3 Nights
  • Difficulty: Class III - IV (Intermediate)
  • Min Age: 8 Years
  • Rate: $1,250 / person

Hell's Canyon Snake River Run

Venture deep into North America's deepest river gorge with massive, roaring whitewater. Tackle legendary Class IV-V rapids including Wild Sheep and Granite Creek, framed by towering 8,000-foot volcanic basalt walls.

  • Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights
  • Difficulty: Class IV - V (Advanced)
  • Min Age: 12 Years
  • Rate: $1,450 / person

Lochsa River Whitewater Blast

An intense, non-stop thrill ride on Idaho's premier free-flowing river. Tackle over 40 named roller-coaster Class IV rapids in an exhilarating single-day journey led by our senior paddle captains.

  • Duration: Full Day (8 Hours)
  • Difficulty: Class IV (High Adrenaline)
  • Min Age: 14 Years
  • Rate: $275 / person
